# Embryo Sketch Animator

Embryo Sketch Animator is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Pencil2D](https://github.com/pencil2d/pencil) (3f1e1d4, commit 3f1e1d4). It does hand-drawn animation.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE.QT.TXT, LICENSE.TXT) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
